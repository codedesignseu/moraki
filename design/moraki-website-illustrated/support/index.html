Support

Answers to the questions parents ask most. Can’t find yours? Email us.

Moraki is a notebook, not medical advice. If you’re worried about your baby, contact your doctor.

How do I invite someone?

Open Settings, tap Invite someone, choose Caregiver or Viewer, and share the code.

How do I join a household?

Install Moraki, open Settings and tap Join with a code. Type the code you got.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Moraki saves every entry on your phone and syncs when you’re back online.

How do I turn on night mode?

Open Settings and pick Night mode: Auto, On or Off. Auto runs from 21:00 to 06:00.

How do I export or delete my data?

Open Settings and go to Your data. You can download everything or delete it.

How do I fix a wrong entry?

Tap Undo right after you log it. Later on, open History, tap the entry and edit it.
