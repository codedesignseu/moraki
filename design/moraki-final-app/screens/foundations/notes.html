Notes for the build
What changed from the brief, and why.

Direction G, applied to every page

  • Faded Stone colours: background #F3F2EE, Feed #F7EBC0, Sleep #F4E4DE, Diaper #E1E3F7, Pump #E0EEE7. Text stays #161412.
  • Headings and big numbers use Comfortaa, body text uses Manrope. Both are free Google Fonts with Greek. This replaces the system font in the brief, because G is the approved look.
  • Home follows G: greeting with the logo, the butter Since-last-feed card with Log a feed, and four tiles with a small + badge. One tap opens the log.
  • All log sheets slide up over a dimmed Home, with the type icon and title at the top left and a round close button at the top right (G placement). Kind choices use G big buttons.
  • The tab bar is G: a white floating pill of four round buttons, the active one dark. At night the pill is #23201C and the active button is light.

Where the palette forced a change

  • Night selected chips and segments use #F1EAE0 fill with #171411 text, so chips and segments match.
  • Undo toast at night is light (#F1EAE0) with a dark Undo pill, because night ink is light.
  • Disabled controls: #E4E2DA fill with #5C5852 text by day (5.4:1), #2C2823 with #B8AD9F at night. Shape never changes.
  • Chips are 48 high to meet the touch rule. Rows wrap, so Greek labels never clip.
  • Warning cards (other account, local entries, sign in first, viewer) use the Feed tint by day and #3A3322 at night, with an icon.
  • Chart bars use a deeper step of the tile colours so short bars stay visible on white.

Taken out of G for the main set

  • Bell, Coming up list, rings and donut charts, nap card, Routine and Tips tabs, "like last time" chip, share buttons on cards. None exist in the app.
  • Sample names replaced with Baby, Parent 1, Parent 2 and Grandparent.

Screens with no earlier design, and what they were drawn from

  • Pump, Health note, Medication, Weight, Milk stock, Appointment and Edit entry: the G feed sheet.
  • Sign in, Join variants, Leave or delete, About, Feedback: G page layout with round back button.
  • Report preview and PDF: Insights and Call script. Startup failure and splash: Welcome. Loading skeleton: G Home.

Greek

  • Greek versions are drawn for Home, Feed, Settings and Consent, day mode.
