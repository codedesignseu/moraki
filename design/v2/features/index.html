Features

Everything Moraki does

A shared notebook for your newborn, built to be quick, calm and private. Here’s what’s inside.

Logging

The big four, two taps away

Tap a tile and a short sheet slides up. Pick what happened with big buttons, and Moraki fills in the time.

  • FeedBreast, bottle or both. Side, minutes, millilitres, formula or breast milk.
  • SleepStart a timer when the baby falls asleep, or add a nap afterwards.
  • DiaperWet, dirty or both, with the time.
  • PumpAmount per side, and whether it went to the fridge, the freezer or a bottle.

More to log

And the rest of your baby’s day

  • Weight

    Write it down after a check-up or a home weigh-in.

  • Temperature

    Keep each reading with the time you took it.

  • Medication

    Name, amount and time, so nobody gives a second dose by mistake.

  • Health notes

    A rash, a cough, a question. Write it while you remember.

  • Appointments

    Date, place and the questions you want to ask.

  • Milk stock

    See how much milk you have in the fridge and the freezer.

History

Every entry, in one place

History shows every entry, newest first, with who logged it. Filter by feeds, diapers, sleep or health.

  • HistoryScroll back through any day. Tap an entry to fix it.
  • TrendsTotals by day and by week. Moraki adds things up. It doesn’t judge them.
  • UndoLogged the wrong thing? Tap Undo right away.

Sharing

One notebook for the whole household

  • Invite with a codeSend a short code to a partner, grandparent or nanny.
  • Caregivers and viewersCaregivers log and edit. Viewers see everything but can’t log.
  • Live on every phoneAn entry shows up on every phone in the household within seconds.
  • See who logged itEvery entry shows who added it, so you never have to ask.
Made for 4am

Built for the night shift

Moraki is designed for one hand, half asleep, in the dark.

  • Night modeWarm, dim colours from 21:00 to 06:00, or whenever you choose.
  • Big buttonsLarge numbers and buttons you can hit without looking twice.
  • Works offlineNo signal in the nursery? Moraki saves first and syncs later.
For the doctor

Ready when someone asks

  • Call scriptOne screen with what a doctor or nurse line usually asks, taken from what you logged.
  • PDF reportThe last days on clean pages, to print or send.

Moraki is a notebook, not medical advice. If you’re worried about your baby, contact your doctor.

Privacy

Your baby’s data stays yours

  • Stored in the EU

    Your data lives on servers in the European Union.

  • No ads

    Moraki shows no ads. Not now, not later.

  • No data sold

    We don’t sell your data or hand it to anyone.

  • No analytics

    No trackers and no analytics, in the app or on this site.

  • No account needed

    Start logging right away. Sign in only when you want to share.

  • Export or delete

    Download or delete everything from Settings, whenever you like.
