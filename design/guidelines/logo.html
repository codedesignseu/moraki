Final logo · round two

Together

Two equal arches hold one small dot. Two parents, one little one. For the final version the mark sits on the optical centre, every gap is the same width, and the wordmark spacing is tuned to the stroke.

moraki

Construction

Stroke 10 units with round ends. Arch radius 13. The gap between the arches and the gap around the dot are both 6 units. The mark is drawn on a 100 unit square and mirrors exactly on the centre line.

Clear space
