Notes for submission

iOS 26 builds icons in Apple's Icon Composer from layers, with light, dark, clear and tinted looks. The pack includes the layers as SVG (Ink background, Honey mark) so your developer can build the .icon file. The flat 1024 PNG still works for older Xcode builds.
Android adaptive layers are 108 dp. Only the centre 72 dp shows, and launchers may crop to a 66 dp circle, so the mark stays inside that circle.
The notification icon must be white on transparent. Android colours it in the status bar.
Proposed tagline for the share image: One shared log for both of you. Not final.
