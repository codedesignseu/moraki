Getting started

Is Moraki free?

Yes. Moraki is free to download and free to use. There are no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start logging right away. You only sign in when you want to share the notebook with someone else.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Moraki is on iPhone first. Android comes next.

Which languages does it speak?

English and Greek. Each person in your household can choose their own.

Sharing with your household

How do I invite someone?

Open Settings, tap Invite someone, choose Caregiver or Viewer, and send them the code.

What’s the difference between a caregiver and a viewer?

Caregivers can log and edit. Viewers can see everything but can’t log.

How do I join a household?

Install Moraki, open Settings and tap Join with a code. Type in the code you got.

Can I take an invite back?

Yes. Each code expires, and you can revoke it from Settings at any time.

Privacy and your data

Where is my data stored?

On servers in the European Union.

Who can see my baby’s data?

Only the people you invite into your household.

Do you sell data or show ads?

No. We don’t sell your data, we don’t share it for ads, and Moraki shows no ads.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Open Settings and go to Your data. You can download everything or delete it all.

Everyday use

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Moraki saves each entry on your phone first and syncs when you’re back online.

I logged something wrong. How do I fix it?

Tap Undo right after you log it. Later, open History, tap the entry and edit it.

How does night mode work?

Pick Auto, On or Off in Settings. Auto switches to warm, dim colours from 21:00 to 06:00.

Can I share a report with my doctor?

Yes. Make a PDF of the last days and print it or send it. The call script puts the basics on one screen for a phone call.

Health questions

Will Moraki tell me if something is wrong?

No. Moraki writes things down and adds them up. It doesn’t judge them or give medical advice. If you’re worried about your baby, contact your doctor.

Is Moraki a medical device?

No. It’s a notebook to help you remember and share what happened.
