How it works

Log it once. Everyone knows.

  1. 1

    Log it in two taps

    Tap a tile, pick what happened, and you’re done. Moraki fills in the time for you.

  2. 2

    Everyone sees it

    The feed you just logged shows up on your partner’s phone. Nobody has to ask who fed the baby last.

  3. 3

    No signal? It still works

    Moraki saves every entry on your phone first and syncs it as soon as you’re back online.

What you can log

Feeds, sleep, diapers and pumping

The four you use most sit on the Home screen. Each one opens a short sheet with big buttons.

  • Feed

    Breast, bottle or both. Side, amount and time.

  • Sleep

    Start a timer, or add a nap after it ends.

  • Diaper

    Wet, dirty or both.

  • Pump

    Amount per side, and where the milk went.

And the rest, one tap further

  • Weight
  • Temperature
  • Medication
  • Notes
  • Appointments
  • Milk stock in fridge and freezer
Made for 4am

Made for 4am

Most logs happen in the dark, with a baby in one arm. Moraki is built for exactly that.

  • Night modeWarm, dim colours switch on by themselves from 21:00 to 06:00. You can also turn them on or off yourself.
  • Large textBig numbers and big buttons you can hit half asleep.
  • One handEverything sits within reach of your thumb. Tapped the wrong thing? Hit Undo.

For the doctor

Ready when you call the doctor

Calling a nurse line at 3am is hard enough. Moraki puts what you logged on one screen so you can read it out.

  • Call scriptAge, latest weight, feeds and diapers in the last 24 hours, last temperature, medications and your questions.
  • PDF reportA clear PDF of the last days to print or send to your doctor.

Moraki is a notebook, not medical advice. If you’re worried about your baby, contact your doctor.

Privacy

Your baby’s data stays yours

  • Stored in the EU

    Your data lives on servers in the European Union.

    Moraki shows no ads. Not now, not later.

  • No data sold

    We don’t sell your data or hand it to anyone.

  • No analytics

    No trackers and no analytics, in the app or on this site.

    Start logging right away. Sign in only when you want to share.

    Download or delete everything from Settings, whenever you like.
