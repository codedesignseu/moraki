Moraki

μωράκι, "little one"

A shared notebook for the people looking after a newborn. Log a feed at 3 a.m., and the other parent, the grandparent or the nanny sees it on their phone too.

What it does

  • Feeds, diapers, sleep, pumping, milk stock, weight, temperatures, notes, medication and appointments, as you log them.
  • Everyone in your household sees the same entries, usually within seconds.
  • It works with no signal. Entries are kept on the phone and sent when there is one.
  • A reminder when the next feed might be due, at the interval you choose.
  • A one-page summary to take to an appointment or read out on the phone.
  • In English and Greek.

Your data

  • Stored on your phone, encrypted, and on servers in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Only the people in your household see it.
  • No advertising, no tracking, never sold.
  • Export everything, or delete your account, from Settings.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Moraki records what you type and adds it up. It is not a medical device and it does not tell you what a number means. If you are worried about your baby, contact your doctor or your local emergency number.
