Medical disclaimer

What Moraki is, what it isn’t, and who to ask about your baby’s health.

1. Moraki is a notebook

Moraki is a notebook. It records what you type and adds it up. It is not a medical device, it does not give medical advice, and it is not a substitute for your pediatrician, midwife, nurse or any other health professional.

It doesn’t diagnose anything and doesn’t suggest a treatment.

2. It doesn’t interpret your numbers

It never tells you what a number means: there are no warnings, ranges or scores. Reading those numbers is your clinician’s job. Take them to your appointments, or read them out on the phone.

There are no reference ranges, thresholds or expected values in Moraki, and no colours on a figure. The weight chart shows the weights you recorded, and the change since birth in plain grams. It doesn’t compare them with anything.

The call script and the PDF report show only what you logged.

3. Reminders can fail

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your phone. They are not guaranteed. A reminder can be late or not show at all, for example if notifications are off for Moraki, a Focus mode or Do Not Disturb is on, battery settings stop the app, the phone is off, or the app was deleted.

Reminders, totals and summaries depend on what was logged and on your phone’s settings. They can be late, missing or wrong if an entry was missed, a phone was off or notifications are turned off.

Never rely on a reminder for medication or care. Don’t rely on Moraki alone for anything time-critical, such as when medication is due.

To check your settings, see Reminders aren’t appearing on the support page.

4. In an emergency

In an emergency, call 112 in Cyprus or Greece, or your local emergency number wherever you are. 112 also works across the EU.

If you are worried about your baby now, contact your doctor or your local emergency number. Do not wait for an appointment.

5. Talk to your doctor

For any question about your baby’s health, talk to your doctor or pediatrician.

Take the figures to your next appointment, or read them out on the phone. The call script is made for that.
