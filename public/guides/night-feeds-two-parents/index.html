Night feeds with two parents: a simple handover

At 3 a.m. the hardest question is rarely how to feed the baby. It’s what already happened while you were asleep.

By Makis, Moraki’s founder and a dad in CyprusUpdated 24 November 20265 min read

In this guide

  1. Agree on the night before it starts
  2. Whoever does it, logs it
  3. Hand over three facts
  4. Keep the screen dim
  5. Look at the night together in the morning

1Agree on the night before it starts

Tired people make decisions badly, so make the plan at dinner, not at 2 a.m. Decide who covers which hours, and what “covering” means: feeding, changing, settling, or all three.

Some couples split the night in two halves. Others take turns feed by feed. There is no right answer, only the one you both remember.

2Whoever does it, logs it

The handover only works if the notebook is up to date. Keep one simple rule: the person who feeds, changes or pumps writes it down straight away.

In Moraki that’s two taps. The time is already filled in, and the form opens with the amount from the last feed, so most entries need no typing at all.

3Hand over three facts

When you swap, the person coming on only needs three things: when the last feed was, when the last diaper was, and anything you noted.

Moraki’s Home screen shows the time since the last feed, the last diaper and who logged each entry. The next person can read it without waking you.

4Keep the screen dim

A bright screen wakes you up more than the baby does. Night mode switches Moraki to warm, dim colours from 21:00 to 06:00, on its own.

5Look at the night together in the morning

Over breakfast, open the History screen together. It shows the whole night in order, with names. It turns “I think she fed at four?” into a quick look instead of a debate.

Tip

Stick the plan on the fridge or pin it in your shared chat. Keep Moraki for what actually happened, so the two never get mixed up.
