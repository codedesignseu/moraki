1. About this Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the website moraki.app uses cookies and related browser-storage technologies.

It concerns the website, not the encrypted database used by the Moraki mobile application. Mobile-app information is described in our Privacy Policy.

2. Cookies currently used

Based on the website configuration reviewed on 25 October 2026, moraki.app does not currently set first-party or third-party cookies.

The website does not currently use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, third-party behavioural tracking, embedded social-media tracking or externally hosted font scripts.

Our website uses Cloudflare for delivery and security. Technical access and security logs may still be generated by infrastructure providers without placing a tracking cookie on your device.

3. Browser local storage

The website uses a local-storage entry named moraki-theme to remember whether you selected the light or dark display theme.

This preference is stored in your browser and is used to provide your selected appearance. It is not used for behavioural advertising or cross-site tracking.

Because this storage supports an interface preference chosen by the visitor, no separate marketing-cookie consent banner is currently provided for this feature.

4. Managing stored preferences

You can change the website's appearance through the available theme control.

You can also remove the saved preference by clearing the website's browser storage or site data through your browser settings.

Clearing it may restore the website's default appearance.

5. Future changes

If we introduce analytics, advertising, embedded media or other technologies requiring consent, we will update this Policy and introduce the appropriate consent controls before those technologies are used, as required by law.

6. Other personal data

Web hosting and security services may process IP addresses, access times and technical request details for service delivery and security. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
