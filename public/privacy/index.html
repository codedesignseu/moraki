Moraki keeps a record of your baby's care that you and the people you invite can all see. Your entries are stored on your phone and, once you sign in and agree, on servers in the EU. Only people in your household see them. They are never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train any model. There are no analytics and no ads in the app.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller is CE Code Designs Ltd, a company established in Cyprus. Moraki is a product of CE Code Designs Ltd. You can reach us at hello@moraki.app. We have not appointed a data protection officer; write to that address about anything in this policy.

2. What we collect

Your account.

  • Your email address, and the one-time code we email you to sign in. The code expires after 10 minutes.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google instead, that company confirms your email address to us. Apple lets you share a relay address instead of your real one. Google's sign-in token also carries the name and profile picture on your Google account; our authentication provider stores them with your account record, and Moraki does not show or use them.
  • The name other caregivers see on your entries (a first name or a nickname, up to 40 characters), your relation to the baby if you choose one, and your role in the household: owner, caregiver or viewer.
  • A record of your consent to health data being processed: which version you agreed to, when, and when you withdrew it, if you did.

Your baby. The name you type (often a nickname), date and time of birth, and birth weight.

What you log.

  • Feeds (bottle amount and milk type, breastfeeding side and time), diapers, sleep, pumping, milk stock, weight, temperature, health notes, medication and appointments.
  • Each entry also records when it happened, who logged it, who last changed it, and whether it was deleted.
  • Notes, appointment details and questions are free text. We can't know what you type there, so we treat all of it as health data. An appointment can name a doctor or a clinic; that is the clinician's personal data, entered by you.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, it sends a report to our crash reporting service: the type of error, a shortened and redacted error message, where in the code it happened, the app version, and your device model and operating system. Before anything leaves the phone, the app removes who you are, any request or response content and any field it has not explicitly allowed, and replaces quoted text, email addresses and IDs. A report can include a random identifier for this installation of the app. Our crash reporting service is set not to store your IP address, and it keeps reports for 30 days.

Feedback. If you send feedback from Settings, we receive your message (up to 2,000 characters), what it is about, the app version, platform and language, your user ID and the time. It is not linked to your household or your baby. Please leave health details out of it.

On your phone only. Your settings (night mode, language, reminders), changes waiting to be sent, and a record of any change the server refused stay on your phone. Feed reminders are worked out and scheduled on your phone; nothing about them is sent to us. Your session token is kept in your phone's secure keychain.

What we don't collect. No location, contacts, photos, phone number, postal address, payment details or advertising identifier. There are no analytics or advertising tools in the app.

You can use Moraki without an account. Your entries then stay on your phone and nothing about them reaches our servers.

4. Health data and your consent

Everything you log about feeding, diapers, sleep, pumping, weight, temperature, medication, notes and appointments is health data. Every caregiver in a household, including a viewer who only reads, is asked for explicit consent on its own screen before their entries leave their phone. Nothing is pre-ticked, and saying no leaves the app working on your phone.

Until you agree, your entries stay on your phone and are not shared. You still see what others in the household share. The baby's details you enter when you set up a household, like name, birth time and weight, are shared with the household as soon as you set it up, even before you agree.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → “Review consent” → “Withdraw my agreement”. Your new entries then stay on your phone and stop syncing. What you already shared stays with the household, as part of the baby's record, until the household is deleted. While your consent is withdrawn, you can't change or delete the entries you already shared. Withdrawing does not close your account. Deleting your account removes what you shared only if nobody else is in the household. See section 9.

Moraki does not interpret your entries. It adds them up and shows them, with no ranges, warnings or scores, and no decision about you is made automatically.

5. Your baby's data

Moraki is used by adults. Your baby cannot consent, so you consent on their behalf as someone with parental responsibility. Please only add people to your household whom you are happy to share your baby's record with.

6. Who can see it

  • People in your household. Everyone you invite sees the same entries and who logged them. Someone removed from a household loses access to our servers straight away, but a copy already on their phone stays there; we cannot reach it.
  • The service providers below, who process data for us under data processing terms and only to run Moraki.

Nothing is sold, used for advertising or used to train any model.

9. Your rights, and how to use them in the app

  • Get a copy (access and portability). Settings → “Data export” → “Export my data” makes two files on your phone, a spreadsheet (CSV) and a fuller one (JSON). The spreadsheet holds the current baby's entries. The fuller file also holds the baby's details and the names and roles of the people in your household. Deleted entries and email addresses are not included. Feedback you sent is not in that export; email us and we will send it.
  • Correct it. Every entry can be edited, and the baby's details can be changed by anyone in the household who logs entries.
  • Delete it. In Settings → “Leave or delete” you can:
    • leave a household that others share; the household and its entries carry on without you;
    • delete a household, if you are its owner: the baby, every entry and every invite are deleted for everyone;
    • delete your account: your email address, sign-in details, sessions, name and role in every household, consent records and feedback are deleted. A household where you are the only member is deleted with everything in it. A household others share stays, because its entries are their record of their baby too: the entries you logged remain and show as logged by someone who has left, and if you were the only owner, you choose who becomes owner before you go. If you signed in with Apple, Moraki first asks Apple to remove its access, by revoking your Sign in with Apple token.
    Deletion removes the data from our servers. Copies already on someone's phone, and files anyone exported, stay where they are until removed there.
  • Restrict or object. Withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy → “Review consent” → “Withdraw my agreement”, or stop using the app. There is no profiling or marketing to object to.
  • Anything else. Email hello@moraki.app. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy) or to the data protection authority where you live.

10. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). On our servers, database rules let a person reach only their own household's data, and health data can only be saved by someone who has consented. Your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure keychain.

Entries on your phone are kept in an encrypted database (SQLCipher). Its key is kept in your phone's secure keychain, and your phone's own storage encryption applies too. Please use a passcode on your phone: it is what protects your entries if the phone is lost.

11. Notifications

Feed and appointment reminders are scheduled on your phone. They show at most a time, never a title, a note, an amount, a temperature or any other figure, so nothing about your baby's health appears on a lock screen.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app. If a change affects what you consented to, we will ask for your consent again.
