Support

Answers to the questions parents ask most. Can’t find yours? Email us.

Help with the shared newborn tracker

Moraki is a notebook, not medical advice. If you’re worried about your baby, contact your doctor.

Email hello@moraki.app, or use Settings → Send feedback in the app. Please leave your baby's health details out of your message.

How do other caregivers see my entries?

Sign in, then invite them from Settings → Invite someone. They install Moraki, sign in, and join with the code or the link you send them (Settings → Join household). Each code works once and expires after 7 days.

From then on, everyone in the household sees the same entries, usually within seconds. Moraki works without signal: an entry made offline stays on the phone and is sent when there is a connection again.

An entry isn't showing on another phone

Check that both phones are online and signed in to the same household. If a caregiver has withdrawn consent for health data (Settings → Privacy → Review consent), their entries stay on their phone and don't sync until they agree again.

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, everything stays on your phone. An account is needed to share with other caregivers and to send feedback.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Settings → Data export → Export my data. Moraki makes two files on your phone, one for a spreadsheet (CSV) with your baby's entries, and a fuller one (JSON) that also has the baby's details and the names and roles of the people in your household. It opens the share sheet so you can save or send them. If you're signed in, the JSON also has your own account: your email address, how you sign in, your consent records and the feedback you sent. Deleted entries and other people's email addresses are not included.

How do I leave a household or delete my account?

Both are in Settings → Leave or delete.

  • Leave a household you share with others. It carries on without you.
  • Delete a household if you are its owner. The baby and every entry are deleted for everyone in it.
  • Delete your account. Your account, your name and your consent records are deleted from our servers. A household where you are the only member is deleted with it. In a household others share, the entries you logged stay as part of their record, and ownership passes to someone else if you were the only owner. If you signed in with Apple, Moraki also asks Apple to remove its access.

Deleting can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

No longer have the app? Email hello@moraki.app from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account, or for a copy of your data. We'll ask you to confirm from that address, then do it and tell you, within one month.

If you signed in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, the quickest way is to install Moraki again, sign in with Apple and delete your account in the app. Or write to us from any address with the hidden address Apple made for Moraki (on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Moraki). We'll send a code to that address, which Apple forwards to you; reply with the code to confirm.

Reminders aren't appearing

Check that notifications are on for Moraki in your phone's settings, and that reminders are on in Settings → Reminders.

Can Moraki tell me if something is wrong?

No. Moraki records and adds up what you log; it is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. If you are worried about your baby, contact your doctor or your local emergency number.
