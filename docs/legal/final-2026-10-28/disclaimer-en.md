# Medical Disclaimer — Moraki

**Effective date: 28 October 2026**

## 1. Record-keeping, not medical advice

Moraki is designed to help parents and caregivers organise information about a baby's daily care.

**Moraki is not a medical device, diagnostic service, healthcare provider or substitute for professional medical advice** in its currently described, non-clinical record-keeping purpose. Its regulatory classification ultimately depends on applicable law and its actual intended use, not on this statement alone.

The app does not diagnose diseases, determine whether a baby is healthy, prescribe treatment or recommend medication doses.

## 2. Information entered by users

Information shown in Moraki depends on what users enter. It can be inaccurate, incomplete, delayed or inconsistent, particularly when several people provide care.

We do not independently verify feeding, sleep, temperature, weight, medication or other entries against medical records or examinations.

## 3. Charts, summaries and reports

Moraki may display recorded weights, changes in grams, activity totals, time intervals and summaries. These are descriptive calculations and not medical assessments.

The application does not currently provide diagnostic thresholds, clinical growth percentiles or a medical evaluation of the child's development.

A report exported from Moraki should be treated as a user-created summary to discuss with a qualified healthcare professional, not as a clinical record verified by Moraki.

## 4. Medicines and treatments

Medication fields are for documenting information entered by a caregiver.

Moraki does not check whether a medicine, dose, interval or combination is appropriate for a child.

Always follow instructions from a qualified healthcare professional. Do not change, start or stop medication based solely on information recorded in the app.

## 5. Reminders and notifications

Moraki provides convenience reminders for certain activities and appointments.

Reminders rely on device permissions, operating-system settings and technical conditions. They may be delayed, unavailable or missed.

**Do not rely on Moraki reminders for urgent medication, emergency care or time-critical medical monitoring.**

## 6. When to seek medical help

If you are concerned about a baby's health, feeding, hydration, breathing, temperature, behaviour or any other symptom, contact a qualified healthcare professional.

For an emergency, contact the local emergency service immediately. In Cyprus and throughout the EU, **112** is the emergency number.

Never postpone appropriate medical care because Moraki shows no warning or because a previous entry appears normal.

## 7. Responsibility and applicable rights

Moraki is a personal organisational tool. You remain responsible for seeking appropriate medical advice and checking information before relying on it.

Nothing in this Disclaimer excludes or restricts legal rights or liabilities that cannot be excluded by applicable law.
