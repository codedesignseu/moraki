# Retention policy

**Task:** P4-11. **Written:** 2026-09-26. **Updated:** 2026-10-25, to match
build 7 (the legal update, `legal-update-2026-10-24.md`, round 2).

Storage limitation (Article 5(1)(e)) means naming a period for every category and
being able to point at the mechanism that enforces it. Where a mechanism does
not exist yet, this document says so instead of implying it does.

| Right                             | State                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

| Access, portability (Art. 15, 20) | **Works.** Settings → Data export → Export my data makes a JSON and a CSV file. The CSV has the current baby's entries. The JSON also has the household id, the baby's name, birth date and birth weight, and the members' user ids, display names and roles. Signed in, it adds the person's own account: user id, email, sign-in methods, and the consent records and feedback the server holds for them, read at that moment (marked unavailable when offline). Deleted entries and other members' email addresses are not included (P4-05, P0.10). Anything else goes through the data-request procedure (`docs/compliance/data-requests.md`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Rectification (Art. 16)           | **Works.** Every entry is editable, and the baby's details are correctable by any writer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Restriction (Art. 18)             | **Through withdrawal.** In the app, restriction is withdrawing. **A caregiver** withdrawing the caregiver agreement leaves the household (the agreement screen's "Withdraw and leave the household", or Settings → Leave or delete), and `leave_household` records the withdrawal. **A guardian** withdrawing health-data consent (Settings → Privacy → Review consent) stops their own processing while another guardian in the household still has consent in force: their new entries stay on the phone and stop syncing. When the **last** guardian with consent in force withdraws, the household is **frozen** (`supabase/migrations/20261024210000_household_freeze.sql`, derived by `household_frozen()`, not stored): nobody writes baby data, no invite is made, nobody joins (an invite made before stops working), and members read nothing. Every author can still read their own entries and soft delete them, but not edit them. A guardian giving consent again, or another member declaring and consenting, unfreezes it. The owner can delete it. Nothing already stored is deleted by a withdrawal. What happens to a household that stays frozen is an open decision (`legal-update-2026-10-24.md`, round 2). Wider requests by email: `data-requests.md` |
| Erasure (Art. 17)                 | **Works.** Settings → Leave or delete (P4-06): leave a household, delete a household (owner), or delete the account. A household with no one else in it goes with the account. A shared one stays with the other caregivers, the person's name and auth record removed, and ownership handed to a member the person chooses (decision D3). Their entries there keep an author id that no longer maps to anyone. Deleting an entry empties its content on the server after 30 days. The person's consent records stay for 3 years after the account goes (see below). On deletion the app asks Apple (on iOS) and Google to remove Moraki's access. After an email request the person does that themselves (`data-requests.md`, section 6). Backups hold deleted data for up to 7 days                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Objection (Art. 21)               | withdraw consent, or stop using the app; there is no profiling and no marketing to object to                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## Server backups (decided 2026-10-24)

- **Plan:** production runs on Supabase Pro from launch.
- **Daily backups:** whole-database snapshots, kept for 7 days on the Pro plan according to Supabase's documentation. Confirm in Supabase → Database → Backups.
- **Point-in-time recovery:** not enabled (paid add-on).
- **Deleted data:** it leaves the backups as they age out, at most 7 days after deletion. Data can't be removed from a backup selectively.
- **Privacy policy:** should say this. The lawyer reviews the wording.
- **After a restore:** deletions made since the backup must be applied again, from the erasure log read off production before restoring. How: `data-requests.md`, section 7.

## Why consent records are kept for 3 years

**For legal confirmation.** Decided 2026-10-25 by the owner with the lawyer,
built in `supabase/migrations/20261025090000_keep_consent_records.sql`.

**Purpose.**

- To demonstrate that consent was given, for what, in which version and
  language, and when it was withdrawn (GDPR Art. 7(1)).
- To establish, exercise or defend legal claims about that consent, for
  example a dispute about whether a guardian agreed (Art. 17(3)(e), which
  limits the right to erasure to that extent).

**What is kept.** One row per grant or withdrawal in `consent_events`: the
account's uuid, the purpose (health data, terms, guardian declaration,
caregiver agreement), the text version, the action, which screen's text was
shown (`text_key`), the language, the household id and baby id where the
agreement was about one, when it happened, and when the account was deleted.
No email address and no sign-in details: those live only in `auth.users` and go
with the account. The uuid still singles out one person's records, so this is
**pseudonymous personal data**, not anonymous, and the GDPR still applies to
it.

**Who can see it.** Only the controller, through the Supabase dashboard or the
service role, signed in with MFA, under the access rules in
`data-requests.md` (section 5). Never through the app or the API: the select
policy returns a person's own rows only while their account exists, so once it
is deleted nobody can read them that way.

**How it is erased.** When the account is deleted, by any route, a trigger on
`auth.users` stamps `account_deleted_at` on the person's rows. The daily
`pg_cron` job `purge-old-consent-records` at 03:47 UTC runs
`purge_old_consent_records()`, which deletes every row whose account was
deleted more than `consent_record_retention()` ago. Nobody deletes them by
hand. Backups hold them for up to 7 days more.

**The period.** 3 years, pending legal confirmation, for example whether it
should follow a Cyprus limitation period instead. It is written in one place,
`public.consent_record_retention()`, so changing it is a one-line migration.
